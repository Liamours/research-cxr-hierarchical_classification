iBioMed 2026 Submission Rules and Template
source: https://ibiomed.ugm.ac.id/submission/ (fetched 2026-07-17)
conference: The 6th International Conference on Biomedical Engineering 2026
  (iBioMed), joint with ICITEE, Oct 21-23 2026, Lombok, Indonesia, organized by UGM.
  Accepted/presented papers submitted for possible IEEE Xplore indexing.


TEMPLATE
Format: IEEE Xplore double-column conference format, A4 paper size only.
Length: 4 to 6 pages, including tables, figures, and references.
Template pointer on the iBioMed page links to the generic IEEE template page
(https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates) rather than a
conference-specific file; that is the standard IEEE conference template
(IEEEtran class for LaTeX, or the matching MS Word template). ieee.org
blocked automated fetch (both browser and WebFetch tools returned a WAF
rejection / HTTP 418) when checked from this session, so the exact template
zip contents were not independently verified -- download it manually in a
regular browser before use.
Note: document/latex/main.tex in this repo is NOT this template. It is a
single-column `article`-class scratch file used to typeset a few result
tables from an earlier session; it has no IEEEtran two-column layout and
predates the current PadChest-inclusive corpus (its numbers are stale).


DOUBLE-BLIND ANONYMIZATION RULES (reject-without-review if violated)
- No author, email, or institutional identity anywhere in the paper,
  including title, author block, and headers/footers.
- Self-citations must also be anonymized: list as "Blind year" or
  "Anonymized year", no identifying details.
- No identifying info in document metadata (e.g. Word's Author field).
- Paper must be processable by similarity/plagiarism checking software.


REJECTION CRITERIA (no review)
- Over the 6-page limit.
- Formatting violations, including fonts too small anywhere (this covers
  figures and graphs, not just body text).
- Not anonymous per the rules above.
- Cannot be processed for similarity checking.
- Similarity score over 30 percent.
- Incomplete submission.
- Currently under review elsewhere, or previously published.
- Must be written in English.


SUBMISSION MECHANICS
Submission system: EDAS, https://edas.info/N34888
  (author identity and affiliations are entered in EDAS itself; anonymity
  requirement applies only to the uploaded paper document.)

  Conference ID for PDF eXpress account creation: 71295X
