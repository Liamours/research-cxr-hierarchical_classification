Masked binary cross-entropy (flat condition):

$$
L_{\text{flat}} = \frac{\sum_{c} m_c \cdot \text{BCE}(\sigma(z_c), y_c)}{\sum_{c} m_c}
$$

Hierarchy-consistency penalty, for each is-a edge with parent $g$ and child $c$:

$$
\text{penalty}(g, c) = \text{ReLU}(p_c - 0.5) \cdot \text{ReLU}(0.5 - p_g)
$$

Combined hierarchical loss (HBCE), summed over the 13 is-a edges $E$:

$$
L_{\text{HBCE}} = L_{\text{flat}} + \lambda \sum_{(g,c) \in E} \text{penalty}(g, c), \qquad \lambda = 0.5
$$
