Average precision at the stricter thresholds of 0.25 and 0.5 is not shown in the
table because it is exactly zero for every one of the six mapped findings,
for both conditions: not one of the 118 predicted boxes reached even the 0.25
threshold against its ground-truth box. This is a hard floor in the data, not
a low average produced by a few weak findings pulling the mean down.
Average precision at the 0.1 threshold shown in the table carries a real
signal only for cardiomegaly, reaching 1.0000 for the flat condition and
0.4500 for the hierarchical condition, but this finding has only 11
ground-truth boxes on the test partition and the result should not be
over-interpreted as a robust estimate.

Grad-CAM localization is not usable as evaluated here, for either training
condition, at the intersection over union thresholds standard in the object
detection literature. The classifiers discriminate at the image level, as
reported in subsection 3.1, but their Grad-CAM heatmaps do not reliably point
at the region containing the labeled finding. The hierarchical condition
scores exactly zero intersection over union and zero intersection over
ground-truth box area for pneumonia across all 12 test images with a
pneumonia bounding box, a flat zero rather than merely a low value, suggesting
the hierarchical condition rarely produces a confident, spatially non-degenerate
activation map for pneumonia specifically; this pattern is worth a targeted
follow-up if the localization component is pursued further.

A data-quality concern affecting this evaluation, and not resolved as part of
this draft, is that the ground-truth cardiomegaly boxes in the rescaled NIH
bounding-box file span only 1.2 to 6.2 percent of image area, smaller than
typical published NIH statistics for cardiomegaly, a finding that is normally
large and central given that it corresponds to the visible cardiac silhouette.
The original, un-rescaled NIH bounding-box file and raw images were not
available locally to independently re-verify the rescale that produced the
224 by 224 ground-truth boxes used here. If that rescale contains an error,
the true intersection over union and average precision figures could be
higher than reported in Table 3 above, and this file's provenance should be
checked before the localization numbers in this section are treated as a
definitive benchmark.
