This section reports the classification and localization results for the flat
and hierarchical conditions described in section 3, both computed on the
held-out validation and test partitions of the pooled corpus. Subsection 4.1
reports classification results: overall discrimination, calibration, and
selective prediction metrics, together with per label detail for the
strongest and weakest discriminated findings. Subsection 4.2 reports
localization results against the subset of images with publicly available
bounding box annotations.
